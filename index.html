My Recipe For Tiramisu

Ingredients

  • 1. make two cups of plain coffee and add 1/4 cup of spiced Rum (optional)
  • 2. Set it aside to cool down
  • 3. In a separate bowl add 16oz of mascarpone cheese and then add 1/8 cup of spiced rum.
  • 4. In a separate bowl add 1 cup of heavy cream and 1/4 cup of sugar and whip it until stiff peaks form.
  • 5. Add the whipped cream to the mascarpone cheese and fold it in gently until combined.
  • 6. In a separate bowl add 1 cup of brewed coffee and 1/4 cup of spiced rum (optional) and mix it together.
  • 7. Dip the ladyfingers into the coffee mixture and layer them in a 9x13 inch dish.
  • 8. Spread half of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers and repeat the process with another layer of ladyfingers and the remaining mascarpone mixture.
  • 9. Dust the top with cocoa powder and refrigerate for at least 4 hours before serving.

Instructions

  1. Make the coffee and let it cool.
  2. Whip the cream with sugar until stiff peaks form.
  3. Mix the mascarpone with the rum.
  4. Fold the whipped cream into the mascarpone mixture.
  5. Quickly dip ladyfingers in the coffee mixture and layer them in a dish.
  6. Spread half the mascarpone mixture over the first layer.
  7. Add another layer of dipped ladyfingers and the rest of the mascarpone mixture.
  8. Top with cocoa powder and chill before serving.
